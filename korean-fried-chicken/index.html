KOREAN FRIEDCHICKEN IN ARANI

Fried twice, tossed by hand the moment it comes out, and eaten with your fingers. Five glazes, ₹159 to ₹189, at the Old Bus Stand.

How it is made

TWICE IN THEFRYER

Ordinary fried chicken is fried once. This goes in twice — the first time to cook it through, the second, hotter, to drive the last of the moisture out of the coating. That is the whole trick: a crust dry enough to stay crisp after something wet is poured over it.

The glaze goes on last, in a bowl, tossed by hand while the chicken is still too hot to hold. Sesame over the top. It is not sauced in advance and it is not held under a lamp, which is why it is cooked only once you order it.

Asked at the counter

GOOD TOKNOW

What makes it Korean fried chicken and not ordinary fried chicken?
Two things. It is fried twice — once to cook it, once to drive the moisture out of the crust — and it is tossed in its glaze only after it comes out of the second fry, so the coating stays crisp under the sauce instead of going soft in it.
Which ones are spicy?
Korean Crispy Fried Chicken and Dragon Crispy Fried Chicken are the hot ones. Teriyaki Crispy Fried Chicken, Malai Crispy Fried Chicken, Smokey BBQ Chicken are not — teriyaki is sweet, malai is creamy and the barbecue is smoked rather than hot.
What does it cost?
From ₹159 to ₹189. Every price is on the menu page, and the counter does not add anything to it.
Do you deliver Korean fried chicken in Arani?
Yes — across Arani town and up to about 20 km around it, Paiyur, Sevur, Kalambur, Kannamangalam included. Order on this site and it reaches the kitchen on WhatsApp, or order through Swiggy.
Where do I eat it?
Twin's Golden Cafe, Old Bus Stand, Arani — by the Old Bus Stand. Open every day, 9 AM – 10:30 PM. It is fried when you order, so it reaches the table hot.
Is the glaze on anything else?
Yes: Gochujang Loaded Fries (₹159), Teriyaki Loaded Fries (₹159), Korean Fried Chicken Wrap (₹149).
